How To Play

Your objective is to guess the five-letter word.

When you submit your guess, the program will tell you whether each letter of your guess appears in the solution word.

Here's an example:

GUESS #1: paste
P A S T E
O O X O -

This means that the letters P, A, and T are correct. The answer will look like: PA_T_.

The letter S appears somewhere in the solution, but not as the third letter.

The letter E does not appear in the solution word.

A good second guess might be PARTS. Let's try it:

GUESS #2: parts
P A R T S
O O - O O

Hmm... So the solution is PA_TS. How about PANTS?

GUESS #3: pants
P A N T S
O O O O O

Whew, we got it!

You have six guesses to get it right!

Now you're ready to play!

Game Modes

On the main menu, you'll see the following game modes:

play

Play today's Wordle.

random

Play a random selection from the 2,317 possible solutions.

The solution list runs until October 20, 2027. After that, this will be the default game mode that is run when you enter play.

date

Enter a date between June 19, 2021 and October 20, 2027 in YYYYMMDD format.

help

I think you know what this one does!

quit

Quit the program. See you tomorrow!

Quick Reference

O = Letter is correct.

X = Letter appears in solution word, but is not in the right place.

- = Letter does not appear in solution word at all.
